About Baytna

Homemade Omani food, made with love — from our home to yours.

Our Story

Baytna started with a simple idea: the best meals come from home kitchens, not commercial ones.

What began as a small effort to share home-cooked Omani food has grown into a place where local home cooks and the people who love their food can find each other.
